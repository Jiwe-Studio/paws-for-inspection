Customs Officer Field Manual

Quick Discrepancy Checklist

DENY ENTRY IF ANY OF THE FOLLOWING OCCUR:

🔴 Expired Permit: the "Valid Until" date is before today's customs date.

🔴 Name Mismatch: the permit owner isn't spelled exactly like the passenger's name. Watch for one-letter typos!

🔴 Overweight: the scale reads above the permit's max weight, even by a little.

🔴 Disguised Animal: scrub the crate with the 🧽 Sponge. DENY if the animal isn't the declared species. Provoke Sound can give it away too.

🔴 Forged Seal: switch to the 🔦 Blacklight UV. A genuine seal glows green. A fake stays dark.

🟢 APPROVE: everything checks out.

Rules unlock shift by shift. Your Daily Bulletin always lists what to check.
